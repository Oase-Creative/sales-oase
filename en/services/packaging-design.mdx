# Packaging Design

Your product's packaging is the first physical touchpoint with your customer. It's not just a box—it's a brand experience. Make it count with custom packaging design that reflects your brand identity and creates memorable unboxing experiences.

## Why Custom Packaging Matters

In a competitive marketplace, packaging can be the difference between a product that gets noticed and one that gets overlooked. Great packaging:

- Creates a memorable first impression
- Reinforces your brand identity
- Enhances the perceived value of your product
- Encourages social sharing and word-of-mouth
- Protects your product during shipping

## Design Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discovery">
    We start by understanding your product dimensions, brand identity, and logistical requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Concept Development">
    We create initial design concepts that explore different visual directions and structural options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refinement">
    Based on your feedback, we refine the chosen concept, perfecting the details and layout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Technical Design">
    We create precise dielines and technical drawings ensuring the packaging fits your product perfectly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalization">
    We deliver print-ready files and specifications to your manufacturer for production.
  </Step>
</Steps>
