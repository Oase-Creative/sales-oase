# 3D Modeling

Bring your products to life with photorealistic 3D modeling and rendering. Perfect for showcasing products before production, creating marketing materials, or visualizing concepts that don't exist yet.

## Why 3D Modeling?

3D modeling offers unique advantages for e-commerce businesses:

- **Pre-Production Visualization** - See your product before manufacturing
- **Cost-Effective** - No need for physical prototypes or photography
- **Flexibility** - Show any angle, color, or configuration
- **Marketing Versatility** - Use renders across all marketing channels
- **Consistency** - Perfect lighting and angles every time

## Our Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Briefing">
    We gather product specifications, reference images, and your vision for the final result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Modeling">
    Our 3D artists create accurate, detailed models based on your specifications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Texturing & Materials">
    We apply realistic textures, materials, and finishes to match your product exactly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lighting & Rendering">
    We set up professional lighting and render high-resolution images from multiple angles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post-Production">
    We refine the renders, adjust colors, and prepare final files for your use.
  </Step>
</Steps>
