# Product Video

Video content converts. We create dynamic product videos that demonstrate value, build desire, and help customers understand your product better than static images ever could.

## Why Product Video Matters

Video is one of the most effective forms of content for e-commerce:

- **Higher Conversion Rates** - Products with video convert significantly better
- **Better Product Understanding** - Video shows products in action
- **Increased Engagement** - Video content gets more views and shares
- **SEO Benefits** - Video content improves search rankings
- **Social Media Performance** - Video performs best on all platforms

## Video Formats & Deliverables

We create videos optimized for different platforms:

- **Square Format (1:1)** - Instagram feed, Facebook
- **Vertical Format (9:16)** - Instagram Stories, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
- **Horizontal Format (16:9)** - YouTube, website, marketplace listings
- **Short-Form Content** - 15-60 second videos for social media
- **Long-Form Content** - Detailed videos for product pages

## Video Length Recommendations

- **Social Media** - 15-60 seconds for maximum engagement
- **Product Pages** - 30-120 seconds for detailed information
- **Marketplace Listings** - 30-90 seconds for key features
- **Tutorials** - 2-5 minutes for comprehensive guides
