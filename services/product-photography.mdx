# Product Photography

Capture your product in its best light with our professional photography services. Great product photography is essential for e-commerce success—it's what makes customers click "add to cart" instead of scrolling past.

## Why Professional Product Photography Matters

In e-commerce, customers can't touch or feel your product. Your photography needs to do the selling. Professional product photography:

- **Increases Conversion Rates** - High-quality images build trust and desire
- **Reduces Returns** - Clear, accurate photos set proper expectations
- **Improves SEO** - Optimized images help with search visibility
- **Enhances Brand Perception** - Professional photography elevates your brand
- **Boosts Social Sharing** - Great images get shared more often

## Types of Product Photography

### White Background (Hero Shots)

Clean, professional product shots on white backgrounds are essential for:

- Primary product images on marketplaces
- E-commerce product pages
- Catalog listings
- Print materials

Marketplace requirements, clean product presentation, versatility

### Lifestyle Photography

Show your product in real-world contexts to help customers visualize ownership:

- Product in use scenarios
- Styled environments
- Lifestyle contexts
- Emotional connections

<Card title="Best for" icon="check">
Social media, brand storytelling, higher-end positioning
</Card>

### Detail Shots

Highlight the quality and features that make your product special:

- Close-up details
- Texture and material quality
- Unique features
- Craftsmanship highlights

<Card title="Best for" icon="check">
Product pages, feature highlights, quality demonstration
</Card>

## Our Photography Process

1. **Pre-Production Planning**
   - Product analysis and styling
   - Shot list creation
   - Equipment and setup preparation

2. **Photography Session**
   - Multiple angles and variations
   - Different lighting setups
   - Detail and lifestyle shots

3. **Post-Production**
   - Professional retouching
   - Color correction
   - Background removal (if needed)
   - Image optimization

4. **Delivery**
   - High-resolution files for print
   - Web-optimized versions
   - Multiple formats (JPG, PNG, WebP)
